Questions people actually ask

Do you accept EcoCash and bank transfer?

Yes. EcoCash and bank transfer are the default ways to pay. We invoice and are paid in USD.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

No. Starter, Professional and the Design Sprint start with a 50% deposit and the balance on delivery. E-commerce and Mobile App use milestone payments instead. Business + Care needs no deposit at all.

What if I'm not happy with the design?

Every package includes at least one revision round before final delivery. We work from a written brief you approve upfront, so the final result should already match what you asked for.

How fast is delivery?

Design Sprint: 3–5 days. Starter and Professional sites: 5–10 business days. E-commerce and Mobile App timelines are scoped per project once we know the full brief.

What happens after my site or app launches?

Every project includes 30 days of support after delivery. Business + Care clients get ongoing hosting, updates and WhatsApp support built into the plan for as long as it's active.
